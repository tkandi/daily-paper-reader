## 摘要
视觉—语言—动作（VLA）、视频—动作（VA）以及世界动作模型（WAM）构成了现代具身智能系统的骨干，但其单批次实时推理仍是一个关键瓶颈。延迟主要并非由单个内核的计算所主导，而是源于系统级开销：数千次短时内核启动、跨算子边界的中间数据搬移，以及连续决策之间的冗余重复计算。通用图编译器无法解决这些成本，因为它们孤立地优化单次调用，且缺乏对跨调用状态语义和全局流水线结构的可见性。我们提出 FLASHRT-PAI，这是一种低延迟推理引擎，其核心是模型—硬件流水线协同设计，而非孤立的内核优化。FLASHRT-PAI 结合了三项核心机制：通过深度内核融合与启动重叠消除算子间边界和启动间隙；通过执行契约形式化定义流水线的阶段 DAG、更新频率和命名缓冲区，从而实现跨决策状态复用与选择性刷新；通过执行状态胶囊实现持久化流水线状态的快速快照、恢复与分叉。该系统还支持通过受一致性和延迟约束的内核替换，对未经修改的 PyTorch 模型进行即插即用式加速。我们在 RTX 5090 上评估了六种策略，并在 Jetson AGX Thor 上评估了四种策略。在相同的 16 位精度下，FLASHRT-PAI 比官方编译路径快 1.3—3.7 倍。将流水线协同设计与低精度相结合后，相较于官方即时执行实现，可获得 3.38—12.66 倍的加速。在 LIBERO-10 上，四位 pi0.5 在 Thor 上达到 94.04% 的成功率，在 RTX 5090 上达到 93.32%，而测得的官方 FP16 参考实现为 92.4%。FLASHRT-PAI 已开源，并通过 Hugging Face Kernel Hub 发布；其可选启用的 pi0.5 内核已被 LeRobot 框架采用。

## Abstract
Vision-language-action (VLA), video-action (VA), and world action models (WAMs) form the backbone of modern physical AI systems, yet their real-time single-batch inference remains a defining bottleneck. Latency is dominated not by individual kernel computation, but by system-level overhead: thousands of short kernel launches, intermediate data movement across operator boundaries, and redundant recomputation across consecutive decisions. General-purpose graph compilers cannot address these costs, as they optimize individual calls in isolation and lack visibility into cross-call state semantics and global pipeline structure. We present FLASHRT-PAI, a low-latency inference engine built around model-hardware pipeline co-design rather than isolated kernel optimization. FLASHRT-PAI combines three core mechanisms: deep kernel fusion with launch overlap eliminates inter-operator boundaries and launch gaps; an execution contract formalizes the pipeline's stage DAG, update rates, and named buffers to enable cross-decision state reuse and selective refresh; and execution-state capsules enable fast snapshot, restore, and fork of persistent pipeline state. The system also supports drop-in acceleration of unmodified PyTorch models via parity- and latency-gated kernel replacement. Evaluation covers six policies on RTX 5090 and four on Jetson AGX Thor. At matched 16-bit precision, FLASHRT-PAI is 1.3-3.7x faster than official compiled paths. Combining pipeline co-design with reduced precision delivers 3.38-12.66x speedup over official eager implementations. In LIBERO-10, four-bit pi0.5 achieves 94.04% success on Thor and 93.32% on RTX 5090, compared with 92.4% for the measured official FP16 reference. FLASHRT-PAI is open source, distributed via Hugging Face Kernel Hub, and its opt-in pi0.5 kernels have been adopted into the LeRobot framework.
