## 摘要
强化学习（RL）后训练已成为提升自回归视频模型的关键范式。新兴的视觉语言奖励模型通过提供准确的奖励，进一步增强了后训练效果，但其长链式思维推理显著提高了相关成本，而对长视频的追求也延长了策略训练时间。尽管成本不断上升，现有视频 RL 系统仍采用粗粒度、以阶段为中心的执行方式：在全部奖励评估完成之前，策略训练始终处于停滞状态；由于策略训练内部存在额外屏障，每条轨迹需被重复回放两次；同时还会导致 GPU 间的负载不均衡。为应对这些挑战，我们提出 StageBreaker，一种细粒度的数据依赖驱动调度框架。该框架调度的是 RL 步骤的真实数据流，而非其各个阶段。具体而言，StageBreaker 通过利用奖励可分离的策略训练，使策略训练与奖励评估重叠执行；并借助与损失无关的反向传播，将两次轨迹回放合并为一次。它还通过以更细粒度调度计算来进一步均衡 GPU 负载。实验评估结果表明，相较于以阶段为中心的执行方式，StageBreaker 最多可实现 1.41× 的加速。

## Abstract
Reinforcement learning (RL) post-training has emerged as the key paradigm for improving autoregressive video models. Emerging vision-language reward models further strengthen post-training with accurate rewards, but their long chain-of-thought inference substantially raises the associated costs, while the pursuit of long videos prolongs policy training. Despite these rising costs, existing video RL systems still adopt coarse-grained stage-centric execution, which stalls policy training until all reward evaluation finishes, replays every trajectory twice due to a further barrier within policy training, and induces load imbalance across GPUs. To address the challenges, we propose StageBreaker, a fine-grained data-dependency-driven scheduling framework, which schedules the true dataflow of an RL step rather than its stages. Specifically, StageBreaker overlaps policy training with reward evaluation by exploiting reward-separable policy training, and merges the two trajectory replays into one by leveraging loss-independent back-propagation. It further balances GPU load by scheduling computation at the finer granularity. Our evaluation results show that StageBreaker achieves a speedup of up to 1.41$\times$ over stage-centric execution.
