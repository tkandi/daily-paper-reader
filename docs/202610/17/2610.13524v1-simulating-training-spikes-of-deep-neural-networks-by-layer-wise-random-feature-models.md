## 摘要
大型语言模型（LLM）预训练过程中损失的间歇性“尖峰”现象，在理论和实证层面仍是一个未解之谜。本文表明，具有重尾幂律分布输入的简单二次模型能够复现这些尖峰。我们引入了一个理论框架和模拟算法，利用这类二次模型刻画深度神经网络（DNN）逐层的训练动态。在一个案例研究中，针对一种已知会在 Transformer 语言模型中诱发尖峰的 Adam 超参数配置（其中 $β_2$ 被设定为接近 $1$），我们的模拟能够根据给定的检查点成功预测表征尖峰行为的隆起。此外，我们从观测到的指标中推导出合适的 $β_2$ 调度策略，并在实证上提升了性能。

## Abstract
Intermittent ``spikes'' in loss, during the pre-training of Large Language Models (LLMs), remains a mysterious phenomenon both theoretically and empirically. In this paper, we show that simple quadratic models with heavy-tailed, power-law distributed inputs can replicate these spikes. We introduce a theoretical framework and simulation algorithm to model per-layer training dynamics of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs), using such quadratic models. In a case study on an Adam hyperparameter configuration (where $β_2$ is set close to $1$) known to induce spikes in Transformer LMs, our simulation successfully predicts bumps indicating spiky behavior from a given checkpoint. Furthermore, we derive an appropriate $β_2$ schedule from observed metrics, which empirically improves performance.
