## 摘要
CNet 是一个 C++/CUDA 框架，用于构建深度复值神经网络（CVNN）并通过采用 Wirtinger 导数的梯度下降法优化复函数。复值模型尚未得到充分探索，但在数据本质上为复数的场景中却十分自然——例如 RF/IQ 通信、MRI k 空间、雷达/SAR 和音频频谱——因为相位携带了实值网络会丢弃的信息。CNet 具有物理原生性：网络是在振幅向量上进行的一系列复数（通常为酉）操作的级联，而分类采用玻恩规则测量 p_k = |z_k|^2/||z||^2，而非对实值 logits 施加 softmax。其复值层库使 conv(x,k) = IFFT(FFT(x).FFT(k)) 能够通过信号处理原语进行学习，包括频谱填充的局部核（Pad）、逆离散傅里叶变换（DFT）、模长非线性 |z|^2（CModulus2）、全纯幂 z^M（CPower）和均值池化（MeanPool）；此外还为每一层提供 GPU 内核、GPU 真 Adam 优化器以及低内存推理模式。本文开展了三项研究。（1）基于一种新的 O(N log N) 因果傅里叶混合器构建的全复值 FNet 风格因果语言模型，在 tiny-shakespeare 数据集上以不足一半的训练步数达到了与参数量匹配的实值因果 FNet 相当的性能。我们提出了玻恩规则注意力：一种基于内容的因果混合器，其权重是一个词元相对于另一个词元的量子测量概率 |<Q_k,K_j>|^2——这是首个基于玻恩规则构建的注意力机制。结合旋转位置嵌入和逐词元复值归一化，全复值玻恩规则模型在 tiny-shakespeare 上达到 1.52 nats/char，与 softmax 注意力相当，并比傅里叶混合器提升 0.23。（2，3）两项瓶颈分析——无线电调制分类（RML2016.10a）和相干衍射成像中的傅里叶相位问题——揭示了 CVNN 需要在哪些方面引入新的算子。复值机制能够学习正确的结构；尚待解决的问题是具体且处于算子层面的。代码：https://github.com/crasmarum/CNet

## Abstract
CNet is a C++/CUDA framework for building deep complex-valued neural networks (CVNNs) and optimizing complex functions by gradient descent with Wirtinger derivatives. Complex models are underexplored yet natural where data is intrinsically complex -- RF/IQ communications, MRI k-space, radar/SAR, audio spectra -- and phase carries information real networks discard. CNet is physics-native: a network is a cascade of complex (often unitary) operations on an amplitude vector, and classification is a Born-rule measurement p_k = |z_k|^2/||z||^2 rather than a softmax over real logits. Its library of complex layers makes conv(x,k) = IFFT(FFT(x).FFT(k)) learnable via signal-processing primitives -- spectral-padding local kernels (Pad), the inverse DFT, a magnitude nonlinearity |z|^2 (CModulus2), holomorphic powers z^M (CPower), and mean pooling (MeanPool) -- with GPU kernels for every layer, a GPU true-Adam optimizer, and a reduced-memory inference mode. Three studies. (1) A fully complex FNet-style causal language model, built on a new O(N log N) causal Fourier mixer, matches a param-matched real-valued causal FNet on tiny-shakespeare in under half the steps. We introduce Born-rule attention: a content-based causal mixer whose weights are quantum-measurement probabilities |<Q_k,K_j>|^2 of one token against another -- the first attention mechanism built on the Born rule. With rotary position embeddings and per-token complex normalization, a fully complex Born-rule model reaches 1.52 nats/char on tiny-shakespeare, matching softmax attention and beating the Fourier mixer by 0.23. (2,3) Two bottleneck analyses -- radio-modulation classification (RML2016.10a) and the Fourier phase problem of coherent-diffraction imaging -- isolate where CVNNs need new operators. The complex machinery learns the correct structure; open problems are concrete and operator-level.   Code: https://github.com/crasmarum/CNet
