## 摘要
资产密集型行业中的故障诊断需要基于从地理上分散的客户端站点收集的含噪声敏感数据训练多类别模型。联邦学习能够在不集中数据的条件下实现协同训练，但大多数方法无法为存储在客户端站点的个体记录提供形式化的隐私保证，并且在标签噪声下性能可能下降。为解决这些局限性，我们提出了一种用于联邦多类别分类的差分隐私与分布鲁棒框架，为存储在各客户端的记录提供样本级隐私保护。我们基于采用 Crammer-Singer 损失的联邦 Wasserstein 分布鲁棒多类别目标函数，并使用基于交替方向乘子法（ADMM）的算法进行求解。为保护发布的客户端更新，在中央服务器聚合之前，我们的方法向局部模型和局部分布鲁棒变量的近端线性化客户端更新中注入经校准的高斯噪声，并利用矩会计方法量化累积隐私损失。我们给出了每轮样本级 $(ε,δ)$-差分隐私保证，以及覆盖所有训练轮次的整体组合隐私保证，并同时提供收敛性结果和明确的隐私—效用权衡。基于模拟工业故障数据和 UCI 基准数据集的实验表明，在大多数测试设置下，所提出的方法优于差分隐私联邦经验风险最小化和随机梯度下降基线方法；在由强隐私保护噪声导致的扰动较大时，其优势最为显著。

## Abstract
Fault diagnosis in asset-intensive industries requires multiclass models trained on noisy, sensitive data collected across geographically distributed client sites. Federated learning enables collaborative training without centralizing data, but most approaches do not provide formal privacy guarantees for individual records stored at client sites and can degrade under label noise. To address these limitations, we develop a differentially private, distributionally robust framework for federated multiclass classification that provides sample-level privacy for records stored at each client. We build on a federated Wasserstein distributionally robust multiclass objective with the Crammer-Singer loss, solved using an Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM)-based algorithm. To protect the released client updates, our method injects calibrated Gaussian noise into the proximal linearized client updates of both the local model and the local distributionally robust variable before aggregation at the central server, and we quantify the cumulative privacy loss using the moments accountant. We provide per-round sample-level $(ε,δ)$-differential privacy guarantees and an overall composed privacy guarantee across all training rounds, together with convergence results and an explicit privacy-utility tradeoff. Experiments on simulated industrial fault data and UCI benchmark datasets show that our method outperforms differentially private federated empirical risk minimization and stochastic gradient descent baselines in most tested settings, with the largest gains under strong privacy-induced noise.
