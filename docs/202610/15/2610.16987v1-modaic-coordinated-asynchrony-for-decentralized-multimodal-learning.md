## 摘要
多模态去中心化学习使异构边缘设备能够在不共享原始数据的情况下进行协作，但不同的模态集合会带来相互耦合的计算与通信瓶颈。多模态智能体支持跨模态对齐，但同时需要训练更多模型组件，并在多个模态覆盖层之间共享带宽。在带宽受限时，这些智能体可能成为热点：传输无法赶上聚合窗口，其更新会变得陈旧或代表性不足，从而降低最差智能体的准确率。异步机制消除了全局阻塞，但其本身无法协调本地跨模态交互与及时的对等节点更新交换。我们提出MODAIC（具有交错协调的模态解耦异步机制），这是一个无等待系统，可在异构计算能力和带宽条件下协调本地模态进展、点对点更新传递与聚合。分层异步机制使各模态能够独立推进，同时确保双向特征交换；负载感知的更新获取机制使训练与通信重叠进行，限制并发服务负载，并复用延迟到达的更新；共识感知的对等节点选择则利用轻量级元数据，在无需主动探测的情况下综合考虑更新可用性、对等节点参与情况和损失相似性。我们将MODAIC实现为一个容器化多智能体框架，并在本地和跨区域云测试平台上进行了评估。在AVE和KU-HAR数据集上，MODAIC提升了平均准确率以及多模态最差智能体的性能。在AVE上，当每个智能体的带宽为200 Mbps时，相较于最强基线方法，MODAIC在1000秒时将多模态最差智能体的准确率提高了11.0个百分点。在云测试平台上，其达到目标准确率所需的时间最多减少30%。消融实验表明，较简单的负载均衡和延迟聚合变体仍存在最差智能体准确率差距；压力测试则显示，该方法能够容忍广域网波动以及部分跟踪器不可用的情况。

## Abstract
Multimodal decentralized learning enables collaboration across heterogeneous edge devices without sharing raw data, but differing modality sets create coupled computation and communication bottlenecks. Multimodal agents support cross-modal alignment while training more model components and sharing bandwidth across multiple modality overlays. Under bandwidth limits, these agents can become hot spots: transfers miss aggregation windows, and their updates become stale or underrepresented, degrading worst-agent accuracy. Asynchrony removes global blocking but does not by itself coordinate local cross-modal interaction with timely peer-update exchange. We present MODAIC (Modality-Decoupled Asynchrony with Interleaved Coordination), a wait-free system that coordinates local modality progress, peer-to-peer update delivery, and aggregation under heterogeneous compute and bandwidth. Hierarchical asynchrony lets modalities progress independently while enforcing reciprocal feature exchange; load-aware update fetching overlaps training with communication, limits concurrent serving load, and reuses late arrivals; and consensus-aware peer selection uses lightweight metadata to account for update availability, peer participation, and loss similarity without active probing. We implement MODAIC as a containerized multi-agent framework and evaluate it on local and cross-region cloud testbeds. Across AVE and KU-HAR, MODAIC improves average accuracy and multimodal worst-agent performance. On AVE at 200 Mbps per agent, it improves multimodal worst-agent accuracy by 11.0 percentage points at 1000 seconds over the strongest baseline. On the cloud testbed, it reduces time-to-accuracy by up to 30%. Ablations show that simpler load-balancing and delayed-aggregation variants leave a worst-agent accuracy gap, and stress tests show tolerance to WAN variability and partial tracker unavailability.
