## 摘要
现有联邦智能范式主要关注模型如何训练或聚合，却忽视了一个更根本的问题：哪些信息应当离开客户端？现有方法通常共享用户难以审查的模型更新，或可能泄露敏感信息的数据表征。此外，对于资源受限的客户端而言，生成这些表征的计算成本可能较高。我们观察到，图像分类并不需要重建原始图像所需的全部视觉细节。这表明，客户端可以在保持任务性能的同时共享更少的信息。然而，当服务器无法访问客户端原始数据时，学习应保留哪些信息仍具有挑战性。为应对这一挑战，我们提出了FedPix，该方法学习共享紧凑且可进行视觉审查的图像，而非模型更新或中间特征。其核心思想是利用共享表征本身来学习哪些信息应当保留、哪些信息可以舍弃。通过平衡任务效用与抗重建能力，FedPix使服务器能够在不访问客户端原始数据的情况下改进表征，同时保持较轻量的客户端计算开销。在BloodMNIST、CIFAR-10、FashionMNIST和MNIST上的大量实验表明，FedPix具有较强的抗重建能力，重建PSNR最低可达6.87 dB，合成PSNR最低可达6.74 dB。与此同时，FedPix最多可将客户端运行时间降低96.0%（在CIFAR-10上从1223 s降至49 s），同时保持具有竞争力的分类准确率：在MNIST、FashionMNIST、BloodMNIST和CIFAR-10上分别达到99.18%、89.97%、82.24%和65.63%。

## Abstract
Existing federated intelligence paradigms primarily focus on how models are trained or aggregated, while overlooking a more fundamental question: \textbf{what information should leave the client?} Existing methods often share model updates that users cannot easily inspect, or data representations that may reveal sensitive information. Moreover, generating these representations can be computationally expensive for resource-constrained clients. We observe that image classification does not require all the visual details needed to reconstruct the original image. This suggests that clients could share less information while preserving task performance. However, learning what to preserve is challenging when the server cannot access raw client data. To address this challenge, we propose \textbf{FedPix}, which learns to share compact, visually inspectable images instead of model updates or intermediate features. The key idea is to use the shared representations themselves to learn what information should be preserved and what can be discarded. By balancing task utility and reconstruction resistance, FedPix enables the server to improve the representations without accessing raw client data, while keeping client-side computation lightweight. Extensive experiments on BloodMNIST, CIFAR-10, FashionMNIST, and MNIST demonstrate that FedPix achieves strong reconstruction resistance, with reconstruction PSNR as low as 6.87~dB and synthetic PSNR as low as 6.74~dB. Meanwhile, FedPix reduces client runtime by up to 96.0\% (from 1223~s to 49~s on CIFAR-10), while maintaining competitive classification accuracy of 99.18\% on MNIST, 89.97\% on FashionMNIST, 82.24\% on BloodMNIST, and 65.63\% on CIFAR-10.
