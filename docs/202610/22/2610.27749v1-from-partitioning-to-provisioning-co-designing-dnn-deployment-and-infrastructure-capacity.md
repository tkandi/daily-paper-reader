## 摘要
深度神经网络（DNN）划分通过将模型计算分配到相互连接的设备上，实现分布式推理。现有方法大多侧重于在计算和通信容量固定的基础设施上优化DNN部署。然而，当这些资源无法充分满足推理需求时，仅优化部署是不够的：还必须对支撑性基础设施进行容量配置。关键在于，部署与配置之间存在内在耦合。DNN的划分方式决定了何处需要容量，而容量配置又可能从根本上改变最优部署方案。受这种相互依赖性的启发，我们研究了面向流水线推理的DNN部署与基础设施容量协同设计。尽管这些决策之间存在耦合，我们揭示了一种可高效求解的结构：在无环网络上，该问题可精确地重构为最短路径问题，从而能够在多项式时间内获得全局最优的联合设计。数值评估表明，与将部署和配置分开处理相比，协同设计具有显著优势，凸显了推理驱动的基础设施规划潜力。

## Abstract
Deep neural network (DNN) partitioning enables distributed inference by dividing model computation across interconnected devices. Existing approaches have largely focused on optimizing DNN deployment over infrastructure with fixed computing and communication capacities. Yet when these resources cannot adequately support inference demands, optimizing deployment alone is insufficient: the supporting infrastructure must also be provisioned. Crucially, deployment and provisioning are inherently coupled. How a DNN is partitioned determines where capacity is needed, while capacity provisioning can fundamentally change the preferred deployment. Motivated by this interdependence, we investigate the co-design of DNN deployment and infrastructure capacity for pipelined inference. Despite the coupling between these decisions, we reveal an efficiently solvable structure: on acyclic networks, the problem admits an exact shortest-path reformulation, enabling globally optimal joint design in polynomial time. Numerical evaluations demonstrate the benefits of co-design over treating deployment and provisioning separately, highlighting the potential of inference-driven infrastructure planning.
