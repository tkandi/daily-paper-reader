## 摘要
Adam 凭借出色的优化性能和训练稳定性，被广泛用于训练大语言模型（LLM），但其对一阶和二阶矩进行逐元素估计会带来显著的内存开销。尽管已有众多内存高效的优化器被提出，但其中许多会引入额外的计算开销，或需要额外调节超参数。因此，Adam 仍然是 LLM 预训练中的主流且可靠选择。我们提出 AdamPM（具有池化矩的 Adam），这是一种简单且内存高效的 Adam 变体：它沿行或列维度对相邻的矩条目进行分组，并使用单个共享均值表示每个组。AdamPM 能够很好地沿用 Adam 的超参数设置，并可轻松集成到现有的 Adam 优化器实现中，同时具有更低的内存开销。我们系统研究了对一阶和二阶矩估计进行池化的方法，并发现二阶矩池化是有效的设计。对于组大小为 s 的情况，AdamPM 可将二阶矩的内存占用降至原始需求的 1/s。语言模型预训练、微调和多模态学习上的大量实验表明，AdamPM 在显著降低优化器状态内存的同时，能够保持模型性能和训练速度。我们的代码可见于 https://github.com/jixiaowei95/AdamPM。

## Abstract
Adam is widely used for training large language models (LLMs) owing to its strong optimization performance and training stability, but its element-wise estimation for the first and second moments incur substantial memory overhead. Although numerous memory-efficient optimizers have been proposed, many incur additional computational overhead or require additional tuning of hyperparameters. Consequently, Adam remains a dominant and reliable choice for LLM pre-training. We propose AdamPM (Adam with Pooled Moments), a simple and memory-efficient variant of Adam that groups adjacent moment entries along the row or column dimension and represents each group by a single shared mean. AdamPM can well inherit Adam's hyperparameter settings and is readily integrated into existing Adam optimizer implementations, with less memory overhead. We systematically investigate pooling of both first and second moment estimates and identify second moment pooling as the effective design. With a group size of $s$, AdamPM reduces second moment memory to $1/s$ of the original requirement. Extensive experiments on language model pretraining, fine-tuning and multimodal learning demonstrate that AdamPM maintains model performance and training speed while substantially reducing optimizer-state memory. Our code is available at \url{https://github.com/jixiaowei95/AdamPM}.
