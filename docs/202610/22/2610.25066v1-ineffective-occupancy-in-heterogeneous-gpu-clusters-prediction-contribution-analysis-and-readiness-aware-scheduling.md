## 摘要
在异构 GPU 集群中，GPU 成本高昂且资源稀缺，但将作业放置到某台机器上并不意味着该作业已准备好运行。大量已分配的容量可能在长时间内处于被占用状态，却不产生有效计算，从而进一步加剧计算资源浪费。我们在阿里巴巴 GPU 集群追踪数据上发现，即使 GPU 容量充足且放置已完成，52.70% 的执行记录也从未进入就绪状态。我们将这一现象称为无效占用。在此诊断基础上，并保持放置策略不变，我们提出就绪感知调度（Readiness-Aware Scheduling，RAS）：该方法仅使用准入时可观测的九项属性来预测就绪概率，并据此重新排序准入顺序。在按机器分组的五折划分下，梯度提升模型的 ROC-AUC 达到 0.8187。特征组贡献分析表明，模型评分主要由作业画像、持续时间和 GPU 型号驱动；请求规模组的贡献最小，其后为调度与并发组。以 GPU 资源计，RAS 将无效占用率（即集群 GPU 中处于无效占用状态的份额）从 43.64% 降至 27.15%，并将大规模请求（八块 GPU 及以上）的有效覆盖率从 46.11% 提升至 63.18%，且不牺牲容量利用率。与利用真实标签排序所得的上界相比，RAS 捕获了 65.1% 的可达改进，显著优于仅依据贡献分析结论构建的可解释规则；因此，其收益主要来自对全部九项属性的联合预测，而非组合基于任一单独属性组的规则。这些结果表明，在异构 GPU 集群中，就绪状态是准入决策中一个可测量、可预测且可优化的维度。

## Abstract
In heterogeneous GPU clusters, GPUs are costly and scarce, yet placing a job on a machine does not mean that the job becomes ready to run. A large amount of allocated capacity can remain occupied for long periods without producing useful computation, further aggravating the waste of compute resources. On the Alibaba GPU cluster trace we find that, even when GPU capacity is sufficient and placement is complete, 52.70% of execution records never became ready. We term this phenomenon ineffective occupancy. Building on this diagnosis, and keeping the placement policy fixed, we propose Readiness-Aware Scheduling (RAS): it uses only nine attributes observable at admission time to predict readiness probability and reorders admission accordingly. Under a five-fold split grouped by machine, the gradient-boosted model reaches an ROC-AUC of 0.8187. A feature-group contribution analysis shows that the model's score is driven mainly by job profile, duration, and GPU model, with the request-size group contributing least, followed by the scheduling and concurrency group. In GPU terms, RAS reduces the ineffective-occupancy ratio (the share of cluster GPUs held under ineffective occupancy) from 43.64% to 27.15% and raises the effective coverage of large requests (eight GPUs or more) from 46.11% to 63.18%, without sacrificing capacity utilization. Relative to the upper bound obtained by ordering with ground-truth labels, RAS captures 65.1% of the attainable improvement, well above the interpretable rules built only from the contribution-analysis conclusions; the gain therefore comes mainly from predicting with all nine attributes jointly rather than from combining rules built on any single attribute group. These results indicate that readiness status is a measurable, predictable, and optimizable dimension of admission decisions in heterogeneous GPU clusters.
