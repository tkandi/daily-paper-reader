## 摘要
语言模型能够基于丰富的物品内容、用户历史和反馈进行个性化推荐。然而，使用全文输入评估大量候选项会带来显著的在线计算开销，并限制服务吞吐量。我们提出 DuoLM，一个将紧凑型推荐模型与高效服务运行时相结合的快速高效个性化推荐框架。编码器 LLM 学习可离线复用的紧凑物品表示，而个性化排序器 LLM 则将这些表示与用户历史和反馈相结合以进行推荐。我们利用推荐监督和教师蒸馏对两个模型进行端到端训练。该运行时还通过用于嵌入访问的虚拟词元 ID、共享历史计算以及 CUDA Graph 回放进一步加速推理。基准测试结果表明，我们的框架实现了与专家教师模型相当的推荐质量。在单张 H100 上，其每秒可完成约 26,400 个候选项的评分，吞吐量约为采用 vLLM 和自动前缀缓存部署的教师模型的 60 倍。

## Abstract
Language models can make personalized recommendations from rich item content, user history, and feedback. However, evaluating many candidates with full-text inputs incurs substantial online computation and limits serving throughput. We introduce DuoLM, a framework for fast and effective personalized recommendation that integrates a compact recommendation model with an efficient serving runtime. An encoder LLM learns compact item representations for offline reuse, while a personalized ranker LLM combines them with user history and feedback for recommendation. We train both models end to end with recommendation supervision and teacher distillation. The runtime further accelerates inference through virtual token IDs for embedding access, shared history computation, and CUDA Graph replay. Benchmark results show that our framework achieves recommendation quality comparable to that of the expert teacher model. On a single H100, it delivers approximately 26,400 candidate scores per second, about $60\times$ the throughput of the teacher served with vLLM and automatic prefix caching.
