## 摘要
联邦学习（FL）能够在保护数据隐私的同时实现协同模型训练。然而，非独立同分布（non-IID）数据不可避免地会引入分类器偏差，导致性能显著下降。现有研究主要聚焦于在联邦学习训练完成后对分类器进行校准或重构，以缓解这一偏差。我们发现，全局模型最后一层的表征往往并非建立全局决策边界的最优选择。相反，基于中间层表征构建的分类器能够取得更优性能。这一发现为突破现有范式固有的性能瓶颈提供了关键启示。然而，在没有辅助数据集或先验知识的情况下识别最优层仍然是一项艰巨挑战。本文提出FLARE（Federated Cross-Layer Joint ClAssifier REconstruction，联邦跨层联合分类器重构），从跨层视角重新思考分类器重构，并将岭回归推广为联合利用跨层表征，从而超越任一单独层的性能。具体而言，在联邦学习训练完成后，客户端仅需进行一次前向传播，即可从预定义层中提取类别条件统计量。服务器聚合这些统计量以构建联合跨层岭分类器，其中，自适应耦合系数用于调节跨层相关性，从而促进互补判别信息的融合。在涵盖11个数据集、8种模型架构以及多种数据异质性场景的大量实验中，结果表明，作为一种即插即用的后处理方法，FLARE仅需额外增加一轮通信即可将准确率提升11.35%。

## Abstract
Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training while preserving data privacy. However, non-independent and identically distributed (non-IID) data inevitably introduces classifier bias, leading to significant performance degradation. Existing studies primarily focus on calibrating or reconstructing the classifier after FL training to alleviate this bias. We find that the last-layer representations of the global model are often suboptimal for establishing a global decision boundary. Instead, classifiers constructed based on intermediate-layer representations can achieve superior performance. This finding provides a critical insight into overcoming the performance bottleneck inherent in existing paradigms. Nevertheless, identifying the optimal layer without auxiliary datasets or prior knowledge remains a formidable challenge. In this paper, we propose FLARE (Federated Cross-Layer Joint ClAssifier REconstruction) which rethinks classifier reconstruction from a cross-layer perspective and generalizes ridge regression to jointly exploit cross-layer representations, thereby surpassing the performance of any individual layer. Specifically, after FL training, clients perform only a single forward pass to extract class-conditional statistics from predefined layers. The server aggregates these statistics to construct a joint cross-layer ridge classifier, where an adaptive coupling coefficient regulates cross-layer correlations, thereby facilitating the integration of complementary discriminative information. Extensive experiments across 11 datasets, 8 model architectures, and diverse data heterogeneity scenarios demonstrate that FLARE, as a plug-and-play post-processing method, improves accuracy by 11.35% with only one additional communication round.
