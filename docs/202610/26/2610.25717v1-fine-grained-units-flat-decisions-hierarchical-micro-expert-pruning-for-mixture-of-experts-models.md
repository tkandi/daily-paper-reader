## 摘要
微专家剪枝通过移除路由专家内部相互耦合的通道，降低混合专家（MoE）模型的容量。然而，更细的剪枝粒度本身并不能保证下游能力得以保留，因为相近的语言建模困惑度可能对应显著不同的任务性能。我们将微专家掩码构建重新审视为两项相互独立的决策：在各专家之间分配保留容量，以及在该容量内选择通道。我们提出 UC-RAMP，这是一种显式分离这两项决策的两阶段训练后剪枝框架。CAPE 利用跨领域校准证据，在严格的逐层预算约束下重新分配专家宽度；RAMP 则通过由通道交互代理指标引导的一出一进交换，在固定宽度下优化通道组成。在三种 MoE 模型上、针对 25% 和 50% 的路由专家通道剪枝，UC-RAMP 在六种设置中的五种下，在所评估剪枝方法中取得了最高的十项任务平均准确率。在 50% 剪枝时，相较于最强的受评估基线方法，它在这三种模型上分别提升了 8.19、3.96 和 6.14 个百分点。在单张 A100 GPU 的部署中，进行 25% 剪枝时，相对于未剪枝模型，UC-RAMP 将实测首词元生成时间缩短了 19.7--23.9%，将每个输出词元的生成时间缩短了 20.2--22.3%。代码可见：https://github.com/xzktx003/UC_RAMP。

## Abstract
Micro-expert pruning reduces the capacity of mixture-of-experts (MoE) models by removing coupled channels within routed experts. However, finer pruning granularity alone does not guarantee the preservation of downstream capabilities, as similar language-modeling perplexity can accompany substantially different task performance. We revisit micro-expert mask construction as two distinct decisions: allocating retained capacity across experts and selecting channels within that capacity. We propose UC-RAMP, a two-stage post-training pruning framework that explicitly separates these decisions. CAPE uses cross-domain calibration evidence to redistribute expert widths under an exact layer-wise budget, while RAMP refines channel composition at fixed widths through one-out, one-in exchanges guided by a channel-interaction surrogate. Across three MoE models at 25% and 50% routed-expert channel pruning, UC-RAMP achieves the highest ten-task mean accuracy among evaluated pruning methods in five of six settings. At 50% pruning, it outperforms the strongest evaluated baseline by 8.19, 3.96, and 6.14 percentage points across the three models, respectively. In a single-A100 deployment at 25% pruning, UC-RAMP reduces measured time to first token by 19.7--23.9% and time per output token by 20.2--22.3% relative to the unpruned model. The code is available at https://github.com/xzktx003/UC_RAMP.
