## 摘要
构建适用于印度的高能力语言模型需要可扩展的多语言数据和稳健的评估。我们推出了 Nemotron-Pretraining-Indic-v1，这是一个合成预训练语料库，包含约 2.83 万亿个词元，覆盖全部 22 种印度宪法附表语言的原生文字形式，以及其中 12 种语言的罗马化形式。这是目前公开发布的规模最大的印度语言预训练语料库。借助我们的 GPU 加速流水线，我们翻译经筛选的英文文档，评估翻译质量，并修复低质量输出，同时保留技术内容和文档结构，并维持源数据可追溯性。通过覆盖 12 种语言的持续预训练实验，我们表明，将该合成语料库与自然印度语言文本相结合，相较于公共语料库基线，能够提升知识与推理能力。额外实验显示，数据规模、文字系统覆盖范围以及印度语言训练数据占比会如何影响英语性能。我们还推出了 Indic-LLM-Eval-Suite，这是一个多语言基准套件，用于从对话、指令遵循、推理、知识、代码和翻译等方面评估印度语言模型。该套件整合了现有的印度语言基准，以及对英文基准进行的任务感知翻译；其人工参与流水线采用自动化质量评估、针对性修正和母语者审校。我们利用该套件，针对不同能力和语言，全面评测了当代开放权重的印度语言模型及多语言模型，揭示出不同任务和语言之间存在显著差异。总体而言，这些资源为开发和评估能力更强的印度语言模型提供了可扩展的基础。

## Abstract
Building capable language models for India requires scalable multilingual data and robust evaluation. We introduce Nemotron-Pretraining-Indic-v1, a synthetic pretraining corpus of approximately 2.83 trillion tokens covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages in native script and twelve in romanized form. It is the largest openly released Indic pretraining corpus. Using our GPU-accelerated pipeline, we translate curated English documents, assess translation quality, and repair low-quality outputs while preserving technical content and document structure and maintaining source traceability. Through continued-pretraining experiments across twelve languages, we show that combining our synthetic corpus with natural Indic text improves knowledge and reasoning over a public-corpus baseline. Additional experiments show how data volume, script coverage, and Indic training share affect English performance. We also introduce the Indic-LLM-Eval-Suite, a multilingual benchmark suite for evaluating Indic language models across chat, instruction following, reasoning, knowledge, code, and translation. The suite combines existing Indic benchmarks with task-aware translations of English benchmarks, using automated quality assessment, targeted correction, and native-speaker review in a human-in-the-loop pipeline. We use the suite to comprehensively benchmark contemporary open-weight Indic and multilingual models across capabilities and languages, revealing substantial variation across tasks and languages. Together, these resources provide a scalable foundation for developing and evaluating more capable Indic language models.
