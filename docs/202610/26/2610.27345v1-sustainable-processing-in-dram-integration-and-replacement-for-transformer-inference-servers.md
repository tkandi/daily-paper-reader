## 摘要
Processing-in-DRAM（PID）加速器正成为 GPU 的一种可持续替代方案，用于 Transformer 模型推理。它们将计算移至数据所在位置，缓解内存带宽限制，并降低整体能耗足迹。然而，针对 PID 加速器的碳分析目前仅覆盖有限的硬件和工作负载。在本工作中，我们提出了一个面向 PID 赋能推理服务器的碳建模框架，该框架同时涵盖隐含碳、空闲运行碳和活跃运行碳。我们的评估覆盖了三种 GPU 基线、六种 PID 架构以及八种 Transformer 工作负载。此外，我们提出了一种考虑老化因素的模型，用于在 10 年时间范围内确定 PID 加速器在碳排放意义下的最优更换周期。在所评估的配置中，分析结果表明，在 AI 推理服务器中纳入 PID 最多仅使总隐含碳增加 2.5%，同时可使编码器和解码器 Transformer 工作负载的总碳排放分别最多降低 1.14 倍和 1.04 倍。结果还取决于与 PID 配对的 GPU 选择：由于具有更高的空闲碳和隐含碳，相较于 A100 和 H100 服务器，在高端 HGX-H100 服务器中引入 PID 模块会产生高出 5.4--5.9 倍的总碳排放。与仅采用 A100 的基线相比，在更高端的 GPU 服务器中引入 PID 模块，会使编码器工作负载的总碳-延迟乘积（CDP）增加，而使解码器工作负载的总碳-延迟乘积降低。

## Abstract
Processing-in-DRAM (PID) accelerators are emerging as a sustainable alternative to GPUs for transformer model inference. They bring compute to data, mitigate the memory bandwidth limitation, and reduce the overall energy footprint. Yet, carbon analysis of PID accelerators is covered for only a limited set of hardware and workloads. In this work, we present a carbon modeling framework for PID-enabled inference servers that captures embodied carbon along with idle and active operational carbon. Our evaluation spans three GPU baselines, six PID architectures, and eight transformer workloads. Additionally, we propose an aging-aware model that finds the carbon-optimal replacement period for PID accelerators over a 10-year horizon. Across evaluated configurations, our analysis shows that including PIDs in AI inference servers increases the total embodied carbon by at most 2.5\%, while the total carbon is reduced by up to 1.14$\times$ for encoder and 1.04$\times$ for decoder transformer workloads. The results also depend on GPU selection for pairing, where introducing PID modules in high-end HGX-H100 servers incurs 5.4--5.9$\times$ higher total carbon compared to A100 and H100 servers, due to their larger idle and embodied carbon. Introducing PID modules in higher-end GPU servers, compared to the A100-only baseline, increases (decreases) the total carbon-delay product (CDP) for encoder workloads (decoder workloads).
