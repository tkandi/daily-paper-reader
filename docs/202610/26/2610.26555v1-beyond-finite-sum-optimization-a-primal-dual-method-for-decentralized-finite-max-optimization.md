## 摘要
许多去中心化系统的评判依据是其表现最差的智能体，而非平均性能。我们研究了连通无向网络上的有限最大值优化问题，其中每个智能体仅知晓其自身的光滑、可能非凸的目标函数。二次正则化产生了一种光滑的有限和表示形式，其中包含一个标量对偶变量以及可局部计算的截断权重。我们提出了一种交替原始-对偶梯度跟踪（APDGT）方法，该方法仅使用邻居间通信，无需任何全局或中心化投影。在光滑性和水平有界性假设下，基于局部化李雅普诺夫分析并结合渐消正则化，我们证明了原始迭代变量有界，且其网络平均值的每个聚点均满足 Clarke 驻点条件。以近活跃梯度度量时，该平均序列可在 O(ε^{-3}) 次迭代内包含一个 ε-驻点。合成实验以及在 Fashion-MNIST 上进行的去中心化逻辑回归展示了该方法的收敛行为及其改善最差智能体性能的潜力。

## Abstract
Many decentralized systems are judged by their worst-performing agents rather than their average performance. We study finite-max optimization over a connected undirected network, where each agent knows only its own smooth, possibly nonconvex objective. Quadratic regularization yields a smooth finite-sum representation with a scalar dual variable and locally computable clipped weights. We develop an alternating primal-dual gradient tracking (APDGT) method, using neighbor communication without any global or centralized projections. Under smoothness and level-boundedness assumptions, a localized Lyapunov analysis with vanishing regularization establishes bounded primal iterates and Clarke stationarity of every accumulation point of their network average. The averaged sequence contains an $\varepsilon$-stationary point, measured by near-active gradients, within $O (\varepsilon^{-3})$ iterations. Synthetic tests and decentralized logistic regression on Fashion-MNIST illustrate convergence behavior and potential improvements in worst-agent performance.
