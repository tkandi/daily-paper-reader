## 摘要
深度神经网络（DNN）是许多安全关键系统的核心组成部分，但其仍极易受到比特翻转攻击（BFA）的影响：仅少量存储器级扰动即可显著降低模型准确率。现有防御方法往往带来较大的硬件开销、依赖重新训练，或无法抵御定向翻转攻击。本文提出 BARE-AI，一种可在推理过程中检测、定位并缓解比特翻转攻击的运行时框架。BARE-AI 引入了 AI 性能计数器（APC），即部署于加速器数据通路中的轻量级硬件监视器，用于捕获每层激活值统计特征，如稀疏性、熵、峰度和频谱偏移。这些特征由用于层安全评估的预测单元（PULSE）进行分析；PULSE 是一个紧凑型检测器，以分类器集成的形式进行离线训练，并在芯片上实现为小型神经引擎。为实现可解释性与恢复能力，BARE-AI 提出激活偏移指数（ASI），用于层级故障定位，并采用基于 z 分数的修复方法，将异常权重重置至接近无攻击状态下的层统计特征。在针对 CNN、视觉 Transformer 和大语言模型开展的随机、定向、自适应及基于幅度的比特翻转攻击实验中，BARE-AI 在视觉模型上实现了最高 98% 的检测准确率，在语言模型上达到 74% 至 95%；其可将 CNN 和 ViT 的准确率恢复至接近无攻击状态的水平，并为大语言模型提供部分恢复效果。在 28 nm 工艺下综合实现时，该监控基础设施的能耗开销低于 3%、面积开销低于 4%、延迟开销约为 10%；通过可配置的运行模式，延迟开销可进一步降至约 6%。不同于纠错码——其冗余度会随可容忍翻转次数的增加而增长——BARE-AI 的开销不随攻击强度变化，因此特别适用于自动驾驶系统、能源和医疗等资源受限的安全关键边缘应用。

## Abstract
Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) are integral to many safety critical systems, yet they remain highly vulnerable to bit-flip attacks (BFAs), where a few memory level perturbations can drastically degrade accuracy. Existing defenses incur significant hardware overhead, depend on retraining, or fail against targeted flips. We propose BARE-AI, a runtime framework that detects, localizes, and mitigates BFAs during inference. BARE-AI introduces AI Performance Counters (APCs), lightweight hardware monitors in the accelerator datapath that capture per-layer activation statistics such as sparsity, entropy, kurtosis, and spectral shift. These are analyzed by the Predictive Unit for Layer Security Evaluation (PULSE), a compact detector trained offline as an ensemble of classifiers and realized on-chip as a small neural engine. For explainability and recovery, BARE-AI introduces an Activation Shift Index (ASI) for layer level fault localization and a z-score based repair that resets anomalous weights toward clean layer statistics. Across CNNs, Vision Transformers, and Large Language Models under random, targeted, adaptive, and magnitude based BFAs, BARE-AI achieves up to 98% detection accuracy on vision models and 74% to 95% on language models, restores near clean accuracy for CNNs and ViTs, and provides partial recovery for LLMs. Synthesized at 28nm, the monitoring infrastructure incurs under 3% energy, under 4% area, and about 10% latency overhead, with a configurable operating point that reduces latency overhead to about 6%. Unlike error correcting codes, whose redundancy grows with the number of tolerated flips, BARE-AI's overhead remains constant regardless of attack strength, making it attractive for resource constrained, safety critical edge applications such as autonomous systems, energy, and healthcare.
