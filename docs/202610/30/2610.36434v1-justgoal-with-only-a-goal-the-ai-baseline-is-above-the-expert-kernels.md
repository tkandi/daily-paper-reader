## 摘要
少量 GPU 内核往往决定了大语言模型训练与推理的效率。高性能内核开发通常依赖专家知识，包括人工实现、与通用模型协作，或使用配备领域知识、评估方法和搜索工作流的专用智能体。随着基础模型能力不断增强，我们研究这些知识究竟能带来多大价值。我们提出 JustGoal，这是一种仅提供目标的基线：通用智能体仅获得优化目标和约束条件，不使用特定于内核的基础设施、知识库或预定义工作流，并自行构建评估循环。在 NVIDIA B200 GPU 上，JustGoal 在 10 个算子中的 8 个上优于开源库，各算子的几何平均加速比为 1.14 倍至 2.34 倍。在具有可用对比结果的 9 个算子中，它还在 5 个算子上超过了公开发布的最快专用智能体内核。在使用 Fable 5.1、针对单一形状进行的受控同模型比较中，KDA-0.5 测试框架达到了 JustGoal 性能的 0.95 倍；而针对单一机制的消融实验显示，领域知识或预定义工作流并未带来一致的收益。这些结果表明，专用机制应当以相对于同模型、同范围的仅目标基线所取得的明确增益来证明其合理性，并且应在基础模型发生变化时重新评估。

## Abstract
A small number of GPU kernels often determines the efficiency of large language model training and inference. High-performance kernel development typically relies on expert knowledge through manual implementation, collaboration with general-purpose models, or specialized agents equipped with domain knowledge, evaluation methods, and search workflows. We investigate how much value this knowledge provides as foundation models become stronger. We introduce JustGoal, a goal-only baseline in which a general-purpose agent receives only the optimization objective and constraints, without kernel-specific infrastructure, knowledge bases, or predefined workflows, and builds its own evaluation loop. On NVIDIA B200 GPUs, JustGoal outperforms open-source libraries on 8 of 10 operators, achieving per-operator geometric mean speedups of 1.14x to 2.34x. It also surpasses the fastest publicly released specialized-agent kernel on 5 of 9 operators with available comparisons. In a controlled same-model comparison using Fable 5.1 on single shapes, the KDA-0.5 harness reaches 0.95x of JustGoal's performance, while single-mechanism ablations show no consistent benefit from domain knowledge or predefined workflows. These results indicate that specialized machinery should be justified by clear gains over a same-model, same-scope goal-only baseline and reevaluated whenever the foundation model changes.
