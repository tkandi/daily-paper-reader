## 摘要
AI 计算正逐渐成为规模庞大且快速变化的电力负荷。其对数据中心基础设施和电网的影响不仅取决于总能耗，还取决于其功率消耗如何在亚秒至分钟的时间尺度上变化，包括峰值、爬坡以及加速器之间同步的波动。现有的大语言模型（LLM）能耗基准通常报告每个词元焦耳数等聚合指标，且很少公开底层时间序列测量数据，使得这些功率动态特性难以得到充分表征和建模。我们提出 LLM-PowerTrace，这是一个开放数据集，包含用于 LLM 微调和推理的、具有时间分辨率的逐 GPU 功率轨迹；该数据集在不同的 NVIDIA 平台上以每 100 ms 一次的频率采样，并配有完整的工作负载和系统元数据。对于微调，LLM-PowerTrace 对覆盖广泛模型规模的开放模型家族以及多种微调方法进行了性能剖析，并系统考察了批量大小、序列长度、精度、并行度和检查点保存等因素。对于推理，该数据集涵盖并发度、GPU 频率、KV 缓存与权重量化、提示词和输出长度以及请求到达模式等方面的变化。LLM-PowerTrace 对一组覆盖广泛模型规模的开放模型家族进行了性能剖析；针对微调，系统扫描了批量大小、序列长度、精度、并行度和检查点保存；针对推理，则扫描了并发度、GPU 频率、KV 缓存与权重量化、提示词和输出长度以及请求到达模式。基于这些功率轨迹，我们定义了面向电网的指标，以及用于外推至未测量配置的 AI 计算功率与能耗标度律。总体而言，LLM-PowerTrace 为建模、预测和管理 AI 计算快速变化的电力行为提供了数据集基础。

## Abstract
AI compute is becoming large and fast-changing electrical loads. Its impacts on data center infrastructure and power grids depend not only on total energy consumption, but also on how their power draw moves over sub-second to minute timescales, including peaks, ramps, and synchronized fluctuations across accelerators. Existing energy benchmarks for large language models (LLMs) report aggregate quantities such as joules per token and rarely release the underlying time-series measurements, leaving these power dynamics poorly characterized and difficult to model. We introduce LLM-PowerTrace, an open dataset of time-resolved, per-GPU power traces for LLM fine-tuning and inference, sampled every 100\,ms on varying NVIDIA platforms paired with complete workload and system metadata. For fine-tuning, LLM-PowerTrace profiles open model families spanning a wide range of model sizes and fine-tuning methods, together with controlled sweeps over batch size, sequence length, precision, parallelism, and checkpointing. For inference, it covers variations in concurrency, GPU frequency, KV-cache and weight quantization, prompt and output lengths, and request-arrival patterns. LLM-PowerTrace profiles a set of open model families across a wide range of model sizes, and controlled sweeps over batch size, sequence length, precision, parallelism, and checkpointing for fine-tuning, and it sweeps concurrency, GPU frequency, KV cache and weight quantization, prompt and output lengths, and request arrival patterns for inference. Built upon power traces, we define power-grid-facing metrics as well as AI-compute power\&energy scaling law for extrapolating beyond the measured settings. Together, LLM-PowerTrace provides a dataset foundation for modeling, forecasting, and managing the fast-changing electrical behavior of AI compute.
