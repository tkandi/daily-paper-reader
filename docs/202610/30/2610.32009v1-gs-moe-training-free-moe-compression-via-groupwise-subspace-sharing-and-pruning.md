## 摘要
专家混合（MoE）模型通过稀疏激活降低计算量，但其专家参数的存储仍是主要的内存瓶颈。我们提出 GS-MoE，一种免训练的压缩框架，通过两个压缩维度——共享子空间秩和专家中间层宽度——来处理专家之间及专家内部的冗余。分组低秩表示在保留专家特定投影的同时，捕获专家之间的共享结构；基于 Nyström 方法的神经元压缩则缩减每个专家内部的中间层宽度。对于固定的共享子空间，我们从重构目标中推导出各专家的重构误差降低量，并将其用于指导专家分组。预算采用分层方式进行分配：首先利用无梯度的层重要性评分，指导在各层之间分配秩和宽度预算；随后，由特征值和杠杆度将这些预算分别分配为各组的非均匀秩以及各专家的中间层宽度。实验表明，沿两个维度进行压缩具有互补优势，尤其是在细粒度 MoE 模型的高压缩率场景下。在 DeepSeek-MoE-16B-Base 上，当模型总参数量减少 60% 时，GS-MoE 保留了未压缩模型平均推理性能的约 90%，而 D$^2$-MoE 仅为 73%；同时，GS-MoE 的分布外困惑度低 20%。代码实现见：https://github.com/cbacary/GS-MoE

## Abstract
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models reduce computation through sparse activation, but storing their expert parameters remains a major memory bottleneck. We introduce GS-MoE, a training-free compression framework that addresses redundancy across and within experts through two compression axes: shared-subspace rank and expert intermediate width. Groupwise low-rank representations capture shared structure across experts while retaining expert-specific projections, and Nyström-based neuron compression reduces intermediate width within each expert. For fixed shared subspaces, we derive per-expert reconstruction-error reductions from the reconstruction objective and use them to guide expert grouping. Budgets are allocated hierarchically using a gradient-free layer-importance score to guide the allocation of rank and width budgets across layers, which eigenvalues and leverage then distribute as non-uniform ranks across groups and intermediate widths across experts. Experiments demonstrate the complementary benefits of compression along both axes, particularly at high compression rates on fine-grained MoE models. On DeepSeek-MoE-16B-Base, at a 60% reduction in total model parameters, GS-MoE retains approximately 90% of the uncompressed model's average reasoning performance, compared with 73% for D$^2$-MoE, while achieving 20% lower out-of-domain perplexity. We provide a code implementation at: https://github.com/cbacary/GS-MoE
