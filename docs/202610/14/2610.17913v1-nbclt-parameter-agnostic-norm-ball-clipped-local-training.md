## 摘要
具有多个局部步骤的分布式学习可减少通信，但会加剧漂移；在异质数据和通信约束下，其实际性能往往依赖于经过精细调优的超参数。与此同时，近期的 Muon 类优化器表明，将更新约束在合适的范数球内，为在优化过程中编码几何先验提供了一种简单方法。本文提出 NBCLT（Norm-Ball Clipped Local Training，范数球裁剪局部训练），这是一个同时适用于联邦学习（FL）和去中心化学习（DL）的统一局部训练框架。NBCLT 将预测-校正局部更新规则与到灵活范数球的欧几里得投影相结合，从而能够在灵活的更新几何结构下执行范数球裁剪，同时保持实现简单。在 FL 中，当每轮局部计算预算随训练轮数进行适当缩放时，其无需问题参数的机制在不假设异质性有界的条件下，实现了 $\mathcal{O}(ε^{-2})$ 的通信复杂度和 $\mathcal{O}(ε^{-4})$ 的随机梯度总复杂度。在 DL 中，NBCLT 同时兼容静态和时变混合机制，并且在类似的计算缩放条件下，除固定网络常数和附加的初始化代价外，保持相同的精度指数。

## Abstract
Distributed learning with multiple local steps reduces communication but aggravates drift, and its practical performance often depends on carefully tuned hyperparameters under heterogeneous data and communication constraints. At the same time, recent Muon-type optimizers suggest that constraining updates within suitable norm balls provides a simple way to encode geometric priors into optimization. In this work, we propose NBCLT (Norm-Ball Clipped Local Training), a unified local-training framework for both federated learning (FL) and decentralized learning (DL). NBCLT combines a predict-and-correct local update rule with Euclidean projections onto flexible norm balls, thereby performing norm-ball clipping under flexible update geometries while retaining a simple implementation. In FL, its problem-parameter-free regime achieves $\mathcal{O}(ε^{-2})$ communication complexity and $\mathcal{O}(ε^{-4})$ total stochastic-gradient complexity without assuming bounded heterogeneity, when the per-round local-computation budget is scaled appropriately with the horizon. In DL, NBCLT is compatible with both static and time-varying mixing and preserves these accuracy exponents under the analogous computation scaling, up to fixed network constants and an additive initialization cost.
