## 摘要
我们研究了联邦场景下的受约束、非光滑、非凸随机优化问题，其中客户端只能访问随机函数评估。现有的联邦零阶方法主要将局部零阶更新与模型平均相结合。然而，这些方法难以应对由局部投影更新引起的客户端漂移，以及随机零阶估计中的采样方差。本文提出了一种基于方向聚合的联邦零阶优化框架，并配备两种局部采样方案。具体而言，FedZOO 利用在多个球面查询之间共享的小批量数据构造局部方向，从而控制由数据采样和梯度近似引起的随机误差。相比之下，FedVRZO 由相互独立的样本—方向对构造局部估计器，因此其采样误差可由样本—方向对的数量直接控制。在两种算法中，客户端均计算沿其投影局部轨迹评估得到的方向均值，服务器则利用客户端方向的加权聚合执行一次投影更新。此外，我们分别为 FedZOO 和 FedVRZO 建立了收敛性保证。在黑盒对抗攻击上的实验表明了所提方法的有效性。

## Abstract
We study constrained nonsmooth nonconvex stochastic optimization in federated settings, where clients access only stochastic function evaluations. Existing federated zeroth-order methods primarily combine local zeroth-order updates with model averaging. However, they struggle with client drift induced by local projected updates and sampling variance in stochastic zeroth-order estimates. In this paper, we propose a federated zeroth-order framework based on direction aggregation, equipped with two local sampling schemes. Specifically, FedZOO constructs a local direction from a minibatch shared across multiple spherical queries, thereby controlling the stochastic errors arising from data sampling and gradient approximation. In contrast, FedVRZO forms its local estimator from independent sample--direction pairs, so that its sampling error is controlled directly by the number of sample--direction pairs. In both algorithms, clients compute the mean of the directions evaluated along their projected local trajectories, and the server performs a single projected update using the weighted aggregate of the client directions. Furthermore, we establish convergence guarantees for FedZOO and FedVRZO, respectively. Experiments on black-box adversarial attacks demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed methods.
