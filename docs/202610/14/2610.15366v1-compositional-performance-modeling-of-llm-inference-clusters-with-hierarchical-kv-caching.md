## 摘要
大语言模型（LLM）推理集群的设计空间探索，需要预测硬件与服务策略如何同分层键值（KV）缓存相互作用。仅凭孤立的操作性能剖面无法确定集群延迟：复用会改变计算需求，而 KV 的位置、容量和争用则决定执行顺序。我们提出了一种组合式离散事件模拟器，其核心是将本地服务开销与共享资源及 KV 可用性分离。基于单预填充/单解码（1P-1D）部署校准的计算与传输模型，可在兼容的工作节点间复用，从而无需针对每种配置进行延迟拟合，即可比较不同策略与拓扑。我们针对部署于 1P-1D 和 3P-1D 集群上的 Qwen3-8B 与 Qwen3-32B，使用 SGLang 进行了验证。对于问答任务和采集的编程会话，以平均绝对百分比误差衡量的请求端到端平均延迟误差分别为 1.34% 和 6.36%。CPU 模拟平均将回放周转时间缩短 15.33 倍；真实回放则包含预设的到达间隔。测得的会话亲和性以及模拟的保留策略研究表明，该框架可通过预测计算量、延迟和 KV 占用率的联合作用，为策略选择提供指导。

## Abstract
Design-space exploration of LLM inference clusters requires predicting how hardware and serving policies interact with hierarchical key-value (KV) caching. Isolated operation profiles alone do not determine cluster latency: reuse changes computational demand, while KV location, capacity, and contention govern execution order. We present a compositional discrete-event simulator based on separating local service costs from shared resource and KV availability. Compute and transfer models calibrated on one-prefill/one-decode (1P-1D) deployments are reused across compatible workers, enabling policy and topology comparisons without per-configuration latency fitting. Validation against SGLang covers Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-32B on 1P-1D and 3P-1D clusters. Mean request end-to-end latency error is 1.34% for question answering and 6.36% for captured coding sessions, measured as mean absolute percentage error. CPU simulation reduces replay turnaround by 15.33 times on average; real replay includes prescribed arrival gaps. Measured session-affinity and simulated retention studies demonstrate how the framework guides policy selection by predicting the joint effects on computation, latency, and KV occupancy.
