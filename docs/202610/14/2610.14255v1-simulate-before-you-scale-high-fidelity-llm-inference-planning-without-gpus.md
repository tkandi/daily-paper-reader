## 摘要
大规模部署大语言模型（LLM）推理服务，需要联合选择硬件布局、并行策略、调度策略、准入控制和请求路由，以满足延迟与吞吐量目标。这些决策相互影响：提升缓存局部性的路由决策可能导致负载集中，而仅在系统饱和时才触发的准入阈值会使队列积累得过深，从而降低关键优先级流量的性能。在 GPU 集群上评估候选方案对于迭代探索而言成本过高；而现有预测工具要么忽略批处理与排队动态，要么仅对推理流水线的局部环节进行建模。本文提出 BLIS，这是一种确定性的、仅使用 CPU 的离散事件模拟器，可对完整的推理服务流水线进行建模，包括准入、路由、调度、预填充/解码执行、KV 缓存管理以及抢占。BLIS 将解析基函数与学习得到的缩放系数及开销项相结合，并通过一次全局拟合来预测单步延迟，无需针对每个模型或每种 GPU 进行性能分析。在涵盖 6 个模型和 3 类 GPU 的 36 组实验中，BLIS 的端到端误差中位数为 6.7%，运行速度约为真实执行的 200 倍。在针对 Llama-2-70B 的 420 种服务配置搜索中，BLIS 找到了一个满足平均 TTFT 为 300 毫秒服务等级目标（SLO）的 4-GPU 部署方案；而竞争工具要么需要两倍数量的 GPU，要么推荐了在负载下无法满足要求的配置。两项完全在仿真中演化得到的策略已在真实硬件上得到验证，并被合并至广泛采用的分布式 LLM 服务开源平台 llm-d：其中，一项准入策略可将关键优先级流量的 TTFT P90 最多降低 82%，一项流量控制策略可将过载工作负载下的端到端 P50 延迟降低 32%–48%。

## Abstract
Deploying large language model (LLM) inference at scale requires jointly selecting hardware layout, parallelism strategy, scheduling policy, admission control, and request routing to meet latency and throughput targets. These choices interact: a routing decision that improves cache locality can concentrate load, and an admission threshold that triggers only at saturation allows queues to grow deep enough to degrade critical-tier traffic. Evaluating candidates on GPU clusters is too expensive for iterative exploration, yet existing predictive tools either ignore batching and queueing dynamics or model only fragments of the pipeline. In this paper, we present BLIS, a deterministic, CPU-only discrete-event simulator that models the full inference serving pipeline (admission, routing, scheduling, prefill/decode execution, KV-cache management, and preemption). BLIS pairs analytical basis functions with learned scaling coefficients and overhead terms, fit once globally, to predict step latency without per-model or per-GPU profiling. Across 36 experiments spanning six models and three GPU types, BLIS achieves 6.7% median end-to-end error while running around 200 times faster than real execution. In a configuration search over 420 serving configurations for Llama-2-70B, BLIS identifies a 4-GPU deployment that meets a 300ms mean TTFT service-level objective (SLO), whereas competing tools either require double the GPUs or recommend configurations that fail under load. Two policies evolved entirely in simulation were validated on real hardware and merged into llm-d, a widely adopted open-source platform for distributed LLM serving: an admission policy that reduces critical-tier TTFT P90 by up to 82%, and a flow-control policy that reduces E2E P50 latency by 32-48% on overloaded workloads.
