## 摘要
同步数据并行训练通常为每个 rank 分配相同数量的记录，即使记录成本可能相差多个数量级。对于可变长度模型，这会将输入差异转化为空闲时间，因为每个训练步都需等待其中 rank 本地批次成本最高的一方。我们提出批次成本前瞻（Batch Cost Lookahead，BCL），一种免协调的数据加载策略：该策略缓冲一个有界的 rank 本地窗口，并将其重新划分为记录数固定且估计成本均衡的数据包。BCL 保持训练器的记录数量约定，且无需进行跨 rank 元数据交换或修改运行时。在针对公开工作负载分布的模拟中，启用 BCL 后，每个 rank 的估计批次成本变异性最高可降低 82.8%。在由 8 张 H200 GPU 运行的 13 个 NeMo-RL SFT 语料库上，rank 本地 BCL 的运行速度为 pooled MFFD 的 1.050 至 1.167 倍。在一项内部序列工作负载上，经过编译的 H200 测量结果显示：相较于打包执行，从 1 个节点扩展至 4 个节点时，其加速比为 1.197 至 1.343 倍。我们还识别出一个内存可行性边界：对于高方差或易发生 OOM 的数据，采用 p99 上限可在 BCL 对其余数据进行均衡前限制异常值。这些结果表明，在数据加载器边界即可消除大量可变成本失衡，同时也阐明了何时仍需进行数据准备或采用更广泛协调机制。

## Abstract
Synchronous data-parallel training usually assigns every rank the same number of records, even when record costs vary by orders of magnitude. For variable-length models, this converts input variation into idle time because each step waits for its most expensive rank-local batch. We present Batch Cost Lookahead (BCL), a coordination-free data-loading policy that buffers a bounded rank-local window and repartitions it into fixed-cardinality packs with balanced estimated cost. BCL preserves the trainer's record-count contract and requires neither cross-rank metadata exchange nor runtime modification. In simulation over public workload distributions, enabling BCL reduces per-rank estimated batch-cost variability by up to 82.8%. Across 13 NeMo-RL SFT corpora on eight H200 GPUs, rank-local BCL runs 1.050 to 1.167 times faster than pooled MFFD. Compiled H200 measurements on an internal sequence workload show speedups of 1.197 to 1.343 times over packed execution from one to four nodes. We also identify a memory-feasibility boundary: a p99 cap for high-variance or OOM-prone data bounds outliers before BCL balances the rest. These results show that substantial variable-cost imbalance can be removed at the data-loader boundary, while clarifying when data preparation or broader coordination is still necessary.
