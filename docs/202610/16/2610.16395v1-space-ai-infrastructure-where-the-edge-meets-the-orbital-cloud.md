## 摘要
人工智能（AI）与空间技术的融合，正推动天基智能从星载有效载荷扩展为一种全球性基础设施，本文将其称为空间人工智能基础设施（SpaceAI-Infra）。其前景建立在空间系统的两项独特优势之上：轨道中丰富的太阳能，以及卫星星座的全球覆盖能力。实现这一前景取决于一个根本性的系统问题：应如何设计和编排卫星星座，以提供可与地面数据中心相竞争的、数据中心规模的AI服务？本文给出了一份面向路线图的回答。首先，我们梳理了SpaceAI-Infra历经三个渐进阶段的发展演进：星载AI、空间边缘AI和空间云AI。通过重新审视端到端词元服务所受的空间约束基础，我们推导出关键设计原则，并将其转化为一体化的空间边缘—云架构。以通信为中心的空间边缘由自由飞行的低地球轨道（LEO）星座构成，提供贴近用户的计算能力，并为云端AI服务形成接入与回传网络。作为对边缘的补充，以计算为中心的空间云由编队飞行卫星星座构成，这些星座被组织为空间数据中心，并运行于晨昏太阳同步轨道。案例研究表明，所提出的架构能够为全球数百万并发用户提供数据中心规模的基础模型推理服务。

## Abstract
The fusion of artificial intelligence (AI) and space technologies is extending spaceborne intelligence beyond onboard payloads toward a global infrastructure, here termed Space AI infrastructure (SpaceAI-Infra). Its promise rests on two distinctive advantages of space systems: abundant solar energy in orbit and the global reach of satellite constellations. Realizing this promise hinges on a fundamental systems question: How should satellite constellations be designed and orchestrated to deliver data-center-scale AI services competitive with their terrestrial counterparts? This article develops a roadmap-oriented answer. We first chart the evolution of SpaceAI-Infra through three progressive stages: onboard AI, Space Edge AI, and Space Cloud AI. By revisiting the space-constrained fundamentals for end-to-end token services, we derive enabling design principles and translate them into an integrated space edge--cloud architecture. The communication-centric space edge comprises free-flying LEO constellations that provide user-proximate computing and form the access and backhaul fabric for cloud AI services. Complementing the edge, the computing-centric space cloud comprises formation-flying satellite constellations organized as space data centers in dawn--dusk sun-synchronous orbits. A case study demonstrates that the proposed architecture can support data-center-scale foundation-model inference for millions of concurrent users worldwide.
