## 摘要
在现实场景中，工业流程可能跨越多个工厂，产品也常在多个地点生产，从而导致不同地点间的数据分布具有共享性但并不完全相同。为解决这一问题，我们在分布式异常检测场景中提出了两种联邦方法：i）组合现有核心集（双重子采样，Double Subsampling）；以及 ii）创建新的核心集（联邦子采样，Federated Subsampling）。具体而言，我们将基于记忆库的 PatchCore 算法适配到联邦环境中，从而提升了冷启动性能、对领域偏移和传感器偏移的鲁棒性，以及对不可靠参与方参与的鲁棒性；后者是经典基于网络的联邦学习策略中的常见问题。

## Abstract
In real-world scenarios, industrial processes can span multiple factories and goods are often produced at multiple sites, leading to a shared yet non-identical distribution of data across locations. To address this, we propose two federated approaches for i) combining existing coresets (Double Subsampling) and ii) creating new coresets (Federated Subsampling) in a distributed anomaly detection setting. In particular, we adapt the memory bank based PatchCore algorithm to a federated setting, resulting in increased cold-start performance, resilience to domain- and sensor-shift, and resilience to unreliable actor participation, a common problem in classical, network based federated learning strategies.
