## 摘要
当多个无线边缘服务器与共同的核心服务器通信时，它们的上行传输会产生多址接入瓶颈，影响分布式边缘学习系统的延迟。本文分析了这一瓶颈，并研究了在分层联邦学习（HFL）中使用分数功率控制（FPC）来缓解该问题。利用随机无线模型对边缘服务器的空间部署和无线信道增益进行建模，我们解析地表征了在TDMA聚合策略下的平均核心聚合延迟（CAL）。我们将累积核心聚合延迟表述为一个更新报酬过程，其中每个学习轮次构成一个更新周期，任务完成定义停止时间。这种表示使得在独立同分布服务器选择下，平均累积聚合延迟可以精确分解为期望停止轮次与期望每轮聚合延迟的乘积。然后，我们推导了在分数功率控制下期望每轮延迟的解析上界。数值结果表明，即使适中的FPC指数也能在广泛的部署场景中显著降低CAL。这些结果凸显了分数功率控制是一种简单且与模型无关的机制，用于缓解边缘学习系统中的无线聚合瓶颈。

## Abstract
When multiple wireless edge servers communicate with a common core server, their uplink transmissions create a multiple-access bottleneck that affects the la- tency of distributed edge learning systems. This paper analyzes this bottleneck and investigates the use of fractional power control (FPC) to mitigate it in hierarchi- cal federated learning (HFL). Modeling the spatial deployment of edge servers and wireless channel gains using stochastic wireless models, we analytically character- ize the mean core aggregation latency (CAL) under a TDMA aggregation policy. We formulate the cumulative core aggregation latency as a renewal reward pro- cess, where each learning round constitutes a renewal cycle and task completion defines the stopping time. This representation yields an exact decomposition of the mean cumulative aggregation latency into the product of the expected stopping round and the expected per-round aggregation latency under iid server selection. We then derive analytical upper bounds on the expected per-round latency under fractional power control (FPC). Numerical results demonstrate that even modest FPC exponents substantially reduce CAL across a wide range of deployment scenar- ios. These results highlight fractional power control as a simple and model-agnostic mechanism for mitigating wireless aggregation bottlenecks in edge learning systems.
