## 摘要
深度神经网络（DNN）加速器的效率取决于如何将每个张量操作映射到硬件上。映射规定了计算如何在存储层次结构中进行分块、排序，以及如何在处理单元之间并行化。这些选择构成了一个组合规模庞大的搜索空间，其中高效映射难以寻得。大多数映射会违反硬件约束，而其余大多数映射又未能充分利用硬件。现有映射器要么仅搜索该空间的一部分以保持搜索的可处理性，从而排除了最高效的映射；要么随机采样整个空间，导致其评估的大多数映射不可行。我们提出了IMPACT，这是一种搜索完整空间的映射器。IMPACT将遗传算法与约束感知修复算子相结合，该算子会将每个不可行映射转换为可行映射，而不是将其丢弃。在灵活加速器上，IMPACT在所有DeepBench工作负载中的能量-延迟积（EDP）、延迟和能耗方面均优于最先进的映射器。在计算存储一体化（CIM）加速器上，由于其约束条件使得仅有少量映射可行，相较于此前最强的映射器，IMPACT在五个网络上将EDP降低了66%、延迟降低了47%、能耗降低了18%，并可媲美由专家手工设计的映射。在灵活和高度受约束的加速器上，IMPACT均能自动发现高效映射。IMPACT将以开源形式发布。

## Abstract
The efficiency of Deep Neural Network (DNN) accelerators depends on how each tensor operation is mapped onto the hardware. A mapping specifies how the computation is tiled across the memory hierarchy, ordered, and parallelized across the processing elements. These choices form a combinatorially large search space, where efficient mappings are hard to find. Most violate hardware constraints, and most remaining mappings underutilize the hardware. Existing mappers either search only part of this space to keep the search tractable, excluding the most efficient mappings, or sample the full space randomly, so most mappings they evaluate are infeasible. We present IMPACT, a mapper that searches the full space. IMPACT pairs a genetic algorithm with a constraint-aware repair operator that converts each infeasible mapping into a feasible one instead of discarding it. On a flexible accelerator, IMPACT outperforms state-of-the-art mappers in EDP, latency, and energy on every DeepBench workload. On a compute-in-memory (CIM) accelerator, whose constraints leave few mappings feasible, it reduces EDP by 66%, latency by 47%, and energy by 18% over the strongest prior mapper across five networks, and rivals hand-crafted expert mappings. Across flexible and highly constrained accelerators, IMPACT finds efficient mappings automatically. IMPACT will be released as open source.
